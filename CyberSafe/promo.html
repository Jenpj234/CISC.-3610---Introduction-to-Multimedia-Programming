// Installation Guide

Install CyberSafe
On Your Device

CyberSafe is a PWA — install it once and use it anywhere, even without Wi-Fi.

01

Open the App in Your Browser

Visit the CyberSafe web app using Chrome (Android/Desktop) or Safari (iOS). Make sure you're on the live hosted URL.

Chrome · Safari · Edge
02

Look for the Install Prompt

On Chrome for Android or Desktop, a banner or "Install App" button will appear automatically. On iOS Safari, tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.

Automatic on Chrome
03

Tap "Add to Home Screen"

Confirm the install. On iOS, tap "Add to Home Screen" from the share menu. On Android/Desktop, tap "Install" in the prompt.

iOS · Android · Desktop
04

Launch & Learn Offline

CyberSafe now appears on your home screen like a native app. Open it anytime — it works fully offline after the first visit.

✅ Works Offline
